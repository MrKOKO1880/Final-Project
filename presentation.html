03 / INDIVIDUAL PRESENTATION

Fable Run

A 3D low-poly fairy-tale chase game demo, produced with AI-assisted modelling and a five-minute project direction presentation.

AI / ASSISTED PRODUCTION Model generation · visual variation · technical support

01 / TITLE + IDEA

Fable Run

  • The player enters a fairy-tale world and is chased by a wolf or beast.
  • Core actions: automatic running, lane switching, jumping, sliding, and obstacle avoidance.
  • Project goal: complete a playable game demo with failure, restart, and an ending flow.
  • Production method: Unreal Engine with AI-assisted model generation.
  • Art direction: low-poly fairy tale, natural environments, and a warm, readable world.

02 / INSPIRATION

Fairy tales and low-poly worlds

  • Story inspiration: The Three Little Pigs and Little Red Riding Hood.
  • Visual references: Nintendo series, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, and Animal Crossing.
  • Environment elements: trees, grass, flowers, animals, brick houses, forest paths, and rural buildings.
  • Familiar fairy-tale symbols communicate the world and chase relationship immediately.
  • External references support research only; no copyrighted game artwork is included.

03 / CREATIVE MOTIVATION

Learning through a complete demo

  • Turn a familiar fairy tale into an interactive chase and parkour experience.
  • Use one complete demo to learn game art, level design, animation, interaction, and packaging.
  • Control scope through a low-poly style while maintaining a clear visual identity.
  • Use AI-assisted model generation to reduce modelling time and technical barriers.
  • Prioritise a fully playable demo before visual refinement or additional mechanics.

04 / ARTISTIC PLAN — PATHWAY

Chase parkour in a fairy-tale forest

  • Pathway focus: Game Art.
  • Art style: low-poly, toy-like, handcrafted, and fairy-tale inspired.
  • Environment: forests, grass, flowers, animals, wooden cabins, brick houses, and natural paths.
  • Player and beast remain distinct through silhouette, colour, and movement.
  • Level rhythm: calm introduction → chase begins → obstacles increase → final escape.
  • Complete demo: start screen, tutorial prompt, one level, failure reset, finish result, and ending screen.

05 / TOOLS AND PRODUCTION

UE5, Blueprints, and AI assistance

  • Game engine: Unreal Engine 5; interaction logic: Blueprints.
  • AI-assisted model generation: low-poly bases, environment elements, prop variations, and material directions.
  • Model refinement: Blender for proportion, structure, topology, and texture adjustments.
  • Environment production: modular construction of forests, paths, houses, obstacles, and chase areas.
  • Interface design: Figma for start, tutorial, failure, and completion screens.
  • Human control: visual consistency, model quality, level pacing, and asset licensing.

06 / CORE QUESTIONS

Core Questions

  1. When visual presentation conflicts with interaction clarity, can visual presentation give priority to interaction?
  2. How can gameplay remain engaging while introducing new mechanics? Will traditional parkour become repetitive or boring?
  3. For a first-time complete game developer, is the production difficulty of this demo too high?
